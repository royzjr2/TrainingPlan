18-Week Shredded Runner Program

💪 Strength Training 🏃‍♂️ Running Plan

Program Overview

  • Goal: Build shredded runner physique - marathon endurance + CrossFit capability
  • Duration: 18 weeks with progressive phases (aligned with marathon training)
  • Equipment: Dumbbells (up to 55lbs each) + Bench
  • Workout Length: 35-40 minutes max
  • Structure: 3-4 strength days + Nike Run Club integration

Key Notes

  • RPE 8 = 2 reps left in the tank
  • RPE 9 = 1 rep left in the tank
  • RPE 10 = Maximum effort, no reps left
  • Tuesday & Thursday: Always Nike Run Club workouts (all weeks)
  • Saturday: NRC Long Run OR Lower Hypertrophy - your choice
  • Rest: 2-3 min for heavy lifts, 60-90 sec for moderate, 45 sec for accessories
  • ✓ Check off workouts as you complete them - progress saves automatically!

Program Progression Summary

Phase 1: Weeks 1-4 - Adaptive & Mastery: Build foundation, establish movement patterns, progressive overload

Phase 2: Weeks 5-7 - Functional Strength Build: Lower reps, explosive movements, supersets, CrossFit-style circuits

Phase 3: Weeks 8-10 - Peak Performance: Max strength testing, metabolic conditioning emphasis, integration work

Phase 4: Weeks 11-14 - Power Endurance & Race Prep: Muscular endurance focus, injury prevention, support increasing run volume

Phase 5: Weeks 15-18 - Taper, Maintenance & Race Week: Minimize fatigue, maintain strength, peak for race day!
