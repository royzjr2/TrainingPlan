10-Week Shredded Runner Program

💪 Strength Training 🏃‍♂️ Running Plan

Program Overview

  • Goal: Build strength and muscular endurance while supporting running performance
  • Duration: 10 weeks with progressive overload
  • Equipment: Dumbbells (up to 55lbs each) + Bench
  • Workout Length: 35-40 minutes max
  • Structure: 4 strength days + Nike Run Club integration

Key Notes

  • RPE 8 = 2 reps left in the tank
  • RPE 9 = 1 rep left in the tank
  • RPE 10 = Maximum effort, no reps left
  • Increase weight by 5-10% each week when prescribed
  • Saturday can be Lower Hypertrophy OR your NRC long run - your choice
  • Rest 2-3 min for heavy lifts, 60-90 sec for moderate, 45 sec for accessories
  • ✓ Check off workouts as you complete them - progress saves automatically!

Program Progression Summary

Weeks 1-2: Foundation Phase - Build base and movement patterns

Weeks 3-4: Foundation Phase Peak - Progressive overload and PRs

Week 5: Deload & Recovery

Weeks 6-7: Strength & Hypertrophy Phase - Higher intensity

Week 8: Peak Week - Maximum effort and absolute PRs

Week 9: Deload & Recovery

Week 10: Assessment Week - Test all your gains!
